Some magic is quiet. It doesn't need to announce itself. It can be found in a thoughtful glance, a curious mind, a calm presence, and the quiet confidence of someone who knows there is still so much to discover.

Franz has always carried her own kind of magic—reserved, observant, intelligent, and quietly captivating.
